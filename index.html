Road to Kozani

Traveling to Kozani is straightforward, offering several convenient options. By air, you can fly from your nearest airport, such as Heraklion or Chania, to Thessaloniki (SKG). From there, you may rent a car, book a taxi or Uber, or take the 01X or 01N bus lines to the Central Bus Station “Macedonia.” Bus tickets can be purchased at the Organization of Urban Transportation of Thessaloniki (OASTH/ΟΑΣΘ) kiosks or directly on the bus using the onboard ticket machine. Each ticket costs €2 per person, payable by credit card, without the need to interact with the driver.
From the “Macedonia” Central Bus Station, you can continue your journey to Kozani by bus. Services depart hourly until 22:00, with an average travel time of approximately two hours, delivering you to Kozani’s Central Bus Station, from where your hotel or apartment is easily accessible. Don't forget to inform the travel agent if you are unemployed, person with special needs, or member of family with more than 3 children. We also suggest you to book a ticket with return for more discount (the departure time can always change).
Here is the tranportation agency platform for more information.
If you prefer to drive, keep in mind that traveling via Thessaloniki involves passing through toll stations at Malgara and Polymylos(€1.15 and €2.35), which should be factored into your estimated costs. For Thessaloniki's traffic state you can take information from this website
An alternative route is through Athens, recommended for travelers using their own car. Following the New Egnatia Road to Thessaloniki, you will encounter toll stations at Afidnes (€3.90), Thiva (€4.55), Tragana (€4.55), Agia Triada (€2.10), Lianokladi (€2.05), Sofades (€3.70), Trikala (€3.25), Kalampaka (€0.95), and Siatista (€2.40). This journey spans roughly 444 km, takes about five hours, and is estimated to cost €86.75 for a vehicle consuming 7.5 L/100 km, including approximately €27.45 saved in toll fees.
A really helpful website for estimating the cost can be found here

Adventurous travelers may prefer a road trip: taking the ferry from Crete to the mainland and then driving north to Kozani (~6–7 hours) allows you to soak in the Greek countryside along the way. Each travel option offers its own charm, ensuring your journey to Kozani is as memorable as the destination itself.
